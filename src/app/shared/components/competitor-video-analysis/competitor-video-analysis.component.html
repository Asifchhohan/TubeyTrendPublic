Competitor Insights

Competitor Insights helps you understand what works for other creators in your niche so you can create better, more discoverable content. This guide explains how to use the tool and what actions to take with the results.

What it does

The tool analyzes competitor channels and their top-performing videos to surface patterns in topics, titles, thumbnails, tags, and engagement. It highlights opportunities and content gaps you can exploit.

Key metrics to monitor

  • Views & Watch Time — identify videos that attract viewers and hold their attention.
  • Engagement — likes, comments and shares show audience resonance and virality potential.
  • Titles & Thumbnails — common hooks and visuals that drive clicks.
  • Tags & Descriptions — keywords used for discoverability and ranking.

How to use it

  1. Enter a competitor channel URL or specific video links.
  2. Review the list of top videos and filter by timeframe or topic.
  3. Note recurring themes (formats, length, posting cadence, thumbnail style).
  4. Export keyword/tag lists and adapt the best-performing elements with your unique angle.

Tools & exports

Export keywords, sample titles, and thumbnail examples to your content planning workflow. Use exported tags in uploads or run A/B tests to measure impact.

Practical tips

  • Don’t copy — adapt: take what works and offer a better angle or higher production value.
  • Test short-form (Shorts) and long-form formats to find traffic and retention sweet spots.
  • Monitor competitor comments to discover pain points and frequently asked questions you can answer.

Example

If a competitor’s tutorial repeatedly gets high watch time, produce an updated, clearer tutorial with timestamps, improved visuals, and stronger CTAs — then measure whether it closes the gap.

Get started

Enter a competitor link and run an analysis. Pick one insight to test each week and track performance to iterate and improve your strategy.
