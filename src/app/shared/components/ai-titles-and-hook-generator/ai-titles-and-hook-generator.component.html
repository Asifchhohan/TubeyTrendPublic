Title & Description Generator

The Title Generator focuses on creating short, high-impact titles optimized for search and clicks. This guide covers how to use the generator effectively, what inputs help produce better titles, and tested best practices.

How it works

The generator uses your seed keyword, video angle, and trending keyword signals to produce several title variations ranked for relevance and clickability. It prioritizes keyword placement, emotional triggers, and readability.

What to provide

  • Main keyword (e.g. "budget travel tips")
  • Format (e.g. list, tutorial, reaction, review)
  • Tone & audience (e.g. casual, urgent, professional)

Step-by-step

  1. Enter your main keyword and select the format and tone.
  2. Click "Generate" to see a list of title suggestions ranked by predicted CTR and relevance.
  3. Pick 2–3 top options and compare for clarity and keyword fit.
  4. Optionally, run quick A/B tests (different thumbnails or slight title variants) to see which performs best.

Best practices

  • Keep titles under ~70 characters so they’re not truncated in search results.
  • Place the most important keyword near the start of the title.
  • Use numbers, triggers (How, Why, Secret, Easy) and clear value propositions.
  • Avoid clickbait that misleads viewers—maintain relevance to the content.

Example

Topic: "easy home workouts"

  • Title options:
    • "10 Easy Home Workouts for Busy People"
    • "Quick Home Workouts: 15 Minutes for Busy Schedules"
    • "No-Equipment Home Workout Routine for Beginners"
