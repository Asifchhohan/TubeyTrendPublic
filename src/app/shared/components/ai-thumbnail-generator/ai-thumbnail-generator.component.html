AI Thumbnail Generator

The AI Thumbnail Generator helps you design thumbnails that improve click-through rate (CTR) by recommending visuals, text placement, and color schemes based on high-performing thumbnails in your niche.

How it works

The tool analyzes thumbnails of top videos for your topic and suggests layout, focal point, and text styles. It provides multiple thumbnail mockups and tips for improving visibility on different devices.

Tips for better thumbnails

  • Use a clear subject or face with strong eye contact.
  • Apply high contrast and bold text for readability on mobile.
  • Keep text short (2–5 words) and highlight the hook or value proposition.

Workflow

  1. Enter your video topic and choose a mood (urgent, funny, dramatic).
  2. Review suggested mockups and pick one or export the assets to edit further.
  3. Test two thumbnail variants and measure CTR for 24–48 hours to pick the best performer.

Get started

Upload a sample image or let the AI generate a mockup. Use the suggested layouts as a starting point and customize to match your brand.
