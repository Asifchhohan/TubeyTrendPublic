Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page outlines what we collect, why, and how you can manage your information.

Data collection

We collect information you provide (account, profile, messages) and technical data (device, IP, usage patterns) to operate and improve services.

How we use data

Data is used to personalize recommendations, power analytics, detect abuse, and provide customer support.

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies for session management, preferences, and analytics. Third-party services (analytics, ads) may set their own cookies — see their policies.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers to deliver features (e.g., payment processors) under contractual safeguards.

Data retention & security

We retain data as long as the account is active or as needed for legal reasons. We use industry-standard protections, though no system is 100% secure.

User rights

Users can request export, correction, or deletion where applicable. Contact support for requests.
