Trending Topics Finder

Find timely, high-potential topics across platforms so you can publish videos that catch trends early. This guide explains where to look, what signals to follow, and practical steps to turn trends into watch-worthy content.

How Trending Detection Works

Trends are identified by spikes in search interest, sudden increases in watch time, and rapid social sharing. By monitoring multiple sources you can spot rising topics before they peak.

Where to Look

  • Google Trends: Compare related queries and check the "Rising" list to spot emerging topics.
  • YouTube: Trending feed, autocomplete suggestions, and "shorts" that gain quick views.
  • Twitter / X & Reddit: Real-time conversations, top posts, and viral threads reveal what people are talking about.
  • TikTok: Trending sounds and challenges that can be adapted into Shorts or hooks for longer videos.
  • Niche Communities: Forums, Facebook groups, Discord servers and Q&A sites surface real audience questions and pain points.

Tools to Use

Tools like Exploding Topics, Ahrefs, and SEMrush detect keywords with accelerating search volume and can send alerts so you act early.
